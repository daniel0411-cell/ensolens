Observed November 10, 2026 in the background browser. Editorial explanation only; no new runtime data feed or historical ranking.

- CPC strength probabilities: https://www.cpc.ncep.noaa.gov/products/analysis_monitoring/enso/roni/strengths/
  - Current issue still October 2026. Categories are weak, moderate, strong and very strong; no separate "super" category in this product.
  - Three-month RONI targets, 1991-2020 base; very strong >= +2.0C. Strength does not guarantee regional impacts. Existing October probability table retained unchanged.
- CPC RONI: https://www.cpc.ncep.noaa.gov/products/analysis_monitoring/enso/roni/
  - ERSSTv6, 1991-2020; three-month mean, tropical mean (20N-20S) subtracted and variance adjusted to match original Nino 3.4.
  - Historical episodes require +/-0.5C for at least five overlapping seasons; atmospheric coupling requires other evidence. Recent values may change up to two months after initial publication.
- CPC ONI: https://www.cpc.ncep.noaa.gov/products/analysis_monitoring/enso/oni/v6/
  - Three-month Nino 3.4 anomalies using centered 30-year bases updated every five years. Page states RONI is used for official monitoring/prediction under NWS PNS 26-05.

Interpretation: weekly Nino 3.4 and seasonal RONI cannot be directly substituted. Historical strength comparisons need matching index, dataset version, baseline method and averaging period. No universal "super" threshold or new record claim is asserted.
