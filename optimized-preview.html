Regional Tendencies, Not Local Forecasts
🎿 Skiers & Snowpack

Sierra vs. Northern Rockies

El Niño historically shifts the Pacific storm track southward, favoring abundant heavy snowfall in Mammoth and SoCal resorts, while the Pacific Northwest often sees warmer storms and elevated snow lines.

Analogue: 2015-16 Winter Guide ↗
🌊 California & West

Atmospheric River Risk

An extended subtropical jet stream brings elevated risks of recurring atmospheric river sequences, heightened coastal flood hazards, and rapid reservoir replenishment across Central and Southern California.

High flood sensitivity Impact Notes ↗
🔥 Heating & Energy

Northern Warmth vs. South

A classic El Niño favors above-normal mean winter temperatures across the Northern Plains and Midwest (reducing natural gas heating demand), coupled with cooler, persistent cloudiness in Texas and the Gulf.

Natural gas outlook Regional Lens ↗
🌀 Atlantic Basin

Hurricane Suppression Link

Equatorial Pacific heating drives strong upper-level westerly winds into the Caribbean and Atlantic Basin. This elevated vertical wind shear tears apart organized tropical storm tops, generally dampening basin activity.
