2. CCSR/IRI portal migration and different chart methods

Displayed November 2026, 26 models (15 dynamical, 11 statistical).
Exact publication day not established. Do not label scheduled day as actual issue.
https://forecast.ccsr.columbia.edu/forecasts/enso/methodology
Undated; checked November 23, 2026.
Nine overlapping three-month seasons; first begins in issue month. Conventional
Niño 3.4 plume plus separate limited-model RONI plume. Available models equally
weighted for each season. Phase probabilities: normal distribution centered on
multi-model mean; width uses historical correlation skill and observed seasonal
variability (sigma_obs * sqrt(1-r^2)); rounded whole-percent totals adjusted to100.
Strength consensus: percentage of available models in nine Niño 3.4 ranges;
explicitly "not a calibrated probability", not adjusted for past skill.
Very strong El Niño threshold 2.0°C in this conventional Niño 3.4 strength chart.
Do not substitute this for CPC RONI strength criteria or official phase odds.
Own example 15/20=75% is model agreement only; no current forecast copied.

3. U.S. Drought Monitor
https://droughtmonitor.unl.edu/About/WhatistheUSDM.aspx
Undated explanation. D1–D4 are drought; D0 abnormally dry can precede or follow
drought. Convergence of evidence includes precipitation, streamflow, reservoirs,
soil, vegetation, demand and local expertise. Snapshot, not a forecast.
Thursday release uses previous Tuesday morning cutoff; Wednesday precipitation
is incorporated in the following assessment, not that Thursday's final map.
