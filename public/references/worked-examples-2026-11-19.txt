Three-month Niño 3.4 anomaly minus the 20N–20S tropical mean anomaly,
with variance adjusted to the original Niño 3.4 index.
Source note: recent RONI values can change for up to two months due to filtering.

These selected seasons are not full-event peaks or an all-time ranking.

CPC ONI: https://www.cpc.ncep.noaa.gov/products/analysis_monitoring/enso/oni/v6/
ASO 2023 ONI +1.5 C; same-window RONI +1.0 C.
ONI uses centered 30-year reference periods updated every five years.
The 0.5 C difference is between indices, not temporal cooling.
Only one vintage checked: no actual revision between vintages is claimed.
The ONI heading names ERSSTv6 while a filter note still names ERSSTv5;
this source inconsistency is not resolved or used to infer a revision here.
